PORTAL SERIES BETA RESEARCH
PSBR DISCORD SERVER
Community / Website Content Reference
Updated: September 21, 2026

============================================================
1. SERVER NAME
============================================================

Official/community name used by the project:

Portal Series BETA Research

Common abbreviation:

PSBR

PSBR is the community and research-oriented Discord server associated with Portal 2 beta research and related Source/Valve development research.

============================================================
2. WHAT IS PSBR?
============================================================

Portal Series BETA Research is a community centered around researching, discussing, documenting, and preserving information about beta and development material from the Portal series and related Valve/source-engine content.

The server provides a place for people interested in Portal beta history and related research to communicate, share discoveries, discuss technical information, and work on research projects.

============================================================
3. PRIMARY PURPOSE
============================================================

The general purpose of PSBR is to support Portal-series beta research.

The community can be used for discussion and collaboration involving subjects such as:

- Historical beta builds.
- Development history.
- Unreleased or changed content.
- Technical research.
- Tools used for examining older builds.
- Mapping and level research.
- File formats and game data.
- Preservation and documentation.
- Screenshots, findings, observations, and discoveries.
- Related Source/Valve development research.

PSBR is a community/research space rather than an official Valve support channel.

============================================================
4. COMMUNITY ROLE
============================================================

PSBR provides a shared environment where researchers and interested community members can compare information and discuss historical material.

The server can serve as a place to:

- Share research findings.
- Discuss beta content.
- Ask technical questions.
- Compare versions of game content.
- Collaborate on tooling.
- Discuss maps and game data.
- Document historical information.
- Share useful references and resources.
- Coordinate community research projects.

============================================================
5. PORTAL 2 BETA RESEARCH
============================================================

A major area of interest within PSBR is Portal 2 beta research.

Portal 2 went through substantial development before its final release, and historical builds can contain content, assets, systems, maps, interfaces, and other material that changed or disappeared during development.

PSBR provides a community environment for studying that history.

Research may involve comparing builds, examining files, testing behavior, documenting differences, studying maps, and preserving information about how Portal 2 developed over time.

============================================================
6. COMMUNITY AND COLLABORATION
============================================================

PSBR is designed around community discussion and collaborative research.

Members can contribute different types of knowledge, including:

- Technical knowledge.
- Historical research.
- Mapping knowledge.
- Reverse-engineering or file-analysis knowledge.
- Development-build knowledge.
- Preservation work.
- Documentation.
- Software/tool development.
- General Portal knowledge.

Contributions do not have to come from a single type of researcher.

============================================================
7. CONNECTION TO P2BL
============================================================

The Portal 2 BETA Launcher (P2BL) is a separate software project that is closely associated with the PSBR community.

P2BL was designed with Portal 2 beta research in mind and is intended to be useful to people working with historical builds and related research material.

P2BL can therefore be considered part of the broader tool ecosystem surrounding the PSBR research community, while remaining its own software project.

The launcher may provide links to the PSBR server so users can find the community associated with the project.

============================================================
8. CONNECTION TO OTHER RESEARCH TOOLS
============================================================

PSBR may also be relevant to or host discussion surrounding other Portal/source research tools and projects.

Related projects discussed in the surrounding P2BL ecosystem include:

The community exists to facilitate research and discussion; individual software projects may have their own creators, repositories, licenses, and release policies.

============================================================
9. SHARING RESEARCH
============================================================

Research shared through PSBR can include:

- Screenshots.
- Videos.
- Build comparisons.
- Technical notes.
- Map research.
- File analysis.
- Historical references.
- Development discoveries.
- Tooling information.
- Documentation.
- Links to public resources.

Members should provide appropriate context for research where practical, especially when information is uncertain or based on incomplete evidence.

============================================================
10. PRESERVATION
============================================================

Preservation is an important part of beta research.

Historical information can become difficult to find as websites, files, tools, and old community posts disappear or become inaccessible.

PSBR can function as a community space for keeping research knowledge organized and discussing ways to preserve important historical information.

The server itself should not automatically be treated as the sole permanent archive for every resource; important material may also need independent preservation, documentation, or public archival copies.

============================================================
11. RESEARCH STANDARDS
============================================================

Research discussions benefit from distinguishing between:

- Confirmed information.
- Direct observations.
- Information found in specific builds.
- Documentation from reliable historical sources.
- Community reports.
- Unverified claims.
- Personal interpretations.

When practical, research should identify the relevant build/version and provide supporting evidence.

This makes it easier for other researchers to reproduce, verify, or challenge findings.

============================================================
12. COMMUNITY CULTURE
============================================================

PSBR is intended to be a place for people who are genuinely interested in Portal-series beta research and related technical/historical subjects.

The community benefits from:

- Respectful discussion.
- Sharing useful information.
- Constructive technical conversation.
- Proper attribution.
- Clear distinction between confirmed facts and speculation.
- Cooperation between researchers with different areas of expertise.
