v2.0.9.2 — CS V1.0 Current
  • Complete rewrite of the main player from Python/PySide6 to C#/WPF/.NET 10.
  • Componentized VidPlayer-authored assemblies.
  • Rebuilt C# updater and rollback manager.
  • Real LibVLC PCM visualizer callback path plus FFmpeg fallback.
