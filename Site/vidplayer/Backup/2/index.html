Welcome to VidPlayer HQ

This is the official home for all VidPlayer builds — modern Sonic Fan Tech editions for Windows 7–11 and axel613's legendary retro fork for Windows 95 through XP.

VidPlayer is a fast, lightweight audio player with a focus on playlists, drag & drop, double-click to play, and a Windows 7-style Aero glass look. It feels like installing a cool audio player from a magazine cover-disc in 2004 — but with modern code, modern OS support, and way fewer toolbars.

What's Inside

🎵
Playlists & Drag & Drop

Load your entire music collection, reorder tracks, and manage playlists the way they were meant to be managed.

🪟
Aero Glass Aesthetic

Windows 7-style glass UI with gloss, gradients, and that peak-Vista energy people thought we'd all forgotten.

📊
FFT Visualizer

Bars, waveform-ish, WMP-style, and spectrogram modes. Fully configurable with HEX color presets saved to JSON.

💿
CD Ripping & Playback

Rip your CDs via FFmpeg and play audio CDs with experimental VLC-based playback support.

Two Branches, One Spirit

Modern Editions (Win 7–11)

Built for 64-bit Windows 7 through 11. PySide6/Qt UI, Aero Glass theme, FFT visualizer, CD support, Discord RPC, and regular updates. If you want shiny new features and modern OS support — this is your pick.

axel613's Retro Fork (Win 95–3.1)

Authentic late-90s CD-ROM software vibe for Windows 95, 98, ME, NT, 2000, XP, 7 — and even a Windows 3.1 shareware edition with MIDI and tiny MP3s planned. Classic setup.exe installer, printed manual, and a fake 2000s software box. It's glorious.
